Understanding Wellness

What does mental wellbeing mean?

Mental wellbeing is not just the absence of illness. It is the ability to cope with the normal stresses of life, work productively, and contribute to your community.

For many young Nigerians, mental health is still a topic surrounded by silence and stigma. But your mind deserves the same care as your body.

Signs of good mental wellbeing include feeling able to manage daily challenges, maintaining healthy relationships, having a sense of purpose, and knowing when to ask for help.

Taking care of your mental health is not weakness. It is wisdom.

Everyday Tools

Grounding when things feel overwhelming

When anxiety or stress feels too heavy, grounding techniques can help bring you back to the present moment.

Try the 5-4-3-2-1 method:

  • Name 5 things you can see
  • Name 4 things you can touch
  • Name 3 things you can hear
  • Name 2 things you can smell
  • Name 1 thing you can taste

This simple exercise interrupts the cycle of overwhelming thoughts and reconnects you to your immediate environment. You don't need to have everything figured out. Just start with right now.

Finding Support

How to start a conversation about how you feel

Telling someone you are struggling is one of the hardest things to do. But it is also one of the most important.

You don't need the perfect words. You can start small:

  • "I haven't been feeling like myself lately."
  • "I've been going through something difficult."
  • "Can I talk to you about something?"

Choose someone you trust — a friend, a family member, a counsellor. You deserve to be heard. If you are not ready to talk to someone you know, ENDtheSilence Foundation is here. Reach out at endthesilencefoundation@gmail.com
