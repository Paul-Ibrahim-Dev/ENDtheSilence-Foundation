Who We Are

A listening ear can change everything.

We’re a Nigerian mental health foundation creating room for honest conversations, practical learning and compassionate support.

Our mission

Open conversations around mental health.

ENDtheSilence aims to foster open conversations around mental health by providing a safe, supportive and non-judgemental environment through education and advocacy. We focus on equipping youth and young adults with the skills and resources needed to maintain positive mental wellbeing.

Our vision

A future where no young person feels alone.

We envision communities where care, understanding and early support are within reach.

Our core values

Discipline

Showing up with care and consistency.

Confidentiality

Respecting privacy and each person’s story.

Empathy

Listening without judgement.

Excellence

Providing thoughtful, trusted support.

Timely delivery

Valuing people’s time and responding with intention.
